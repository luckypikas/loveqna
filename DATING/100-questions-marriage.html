100 Questions You Should Always Ask Before Marriage

• Relationship Diagnostics | Lucky Pikas Studios

Love is an emotion. Marriage is a legally binding contract. You are not just choosing a romantic partner; you are choosing your emergency contact, your financial co-signer, and the person who will manage your health decisions.

Most divorces do not happen because of a sudden loss of love. They happen because of a slow, devastating realization of incompatibility in money, family boundaries, and household labor. Before you sign the contract, you must perform absolute due diligence.

Part 1: The Financial Audit (Money & Debt)

  1. What is your exact current credit score?
  2. How much debt (student loans, credit cards, taxes) are you bringing into this marriage?
  3. Do we merge all of our bank accounts, or maintain separate accounts and a joint bill account?
  4. What is the maximum amount of money either of us can spend without consulting the other?
  5. Do you believe in taking out loans for things other than a house or a car?
  6. If my family or your family asks for a loan, how do we handle it?
  7. How much should we save for retirement each month?
  8. Are we signing a prenuptial agreement to protect pre-marital assets?
  9. If one of us loses our job, what is the immediate backup plan?
  10. Who is physically paying the bills each month?
  11. What is your definition of "financial ruin"?
  12. Do you believe in tithing or donating a percentage of our income?
  13. Do you expect me to financially support your parents when they get older?
  14. What is your biggest financial regret so far?
  15. If we win the lottery, what is the very first thing we do with the money?

Part 2: Family Boundaries & In-Laws

  1. How much influence do your parents have over your decisions?
  2. If your mother/father disrespects me, will you defend me publicly?
  3. How often do you expect us to visit your family?
  4. Will our parents have a key to our home?
  5. If we argue, do you discuss the details with your family or friends?
  6. Are we expected to spend every major holiday with your family?
  7. Do you have any family members who are not allowed in our home?
  8. How will we handle it if our parents disagree with how we raise our kids?
  9. Are we expected to take in elderly parents to live with us in the future?
  10. What traditions from your childhood do you want to keep, and which do you want to abandon?

Part 3: Conflict & Crisis Management

  1. When you get overwhelmingly angry, do you need space or do you need to talk immediately?
  2. Have you ever used the "silent treatment" as a form of punishment?
  3. What is an absolute, non-negotiable dealbreaker that would end this marriage instantly?
  4. If we face a severe crisis, do you want to seek professional counseling or therapy?
  5. Is there anything from your past that could suddenly appear and affect our marriage?
  6. Do you believe that threatening divorce during an argument is ever acceptable?
  7. How do you handle apologies? (Do you require them, and can you give them?)
  8. What is the most effective way for me to comfort you when you fail?
  9. Have you fully forgiven the people who hurt you in your past?
  10. If I tell you that something you did hurt my feelings, what is your first instinct?

Part 4: Children & Parenting

  1. Do you absolutely, 100% want children, and how many?
  2. What happens if we find out we are biologically unable to have children?
  3. If we have children, who is staying home, or will we use daycare?
  4. What are your exact views on physical discipline (spanking) for children?
  5. What religion or moral philosophy will our children be raised in?
  6. How will we handle it if our child comes out as LGBTQ+?
  7. How will we fund their education?
  8. Do you believe in strictly monitoring a teenager's internet and phone access?
  9. What is the one thing your parents did to you that you will never do to our child?
  10. If we disagree on a parenting decision, how do we resolve it in front of the kids?

Part 5: Household Roles & Labor

  1. Who is doing the cooking, and who is doing the cleaning?
  2. Do you believe in "traditional" gender roles inside the home?
  3. What constitutes a "messy" house to you?
  4. Who is responsible for the emotional labor? (Remembering birthdays, scheduling doctors, planning trips).
  5. Who handles the outdoor maintenance, car repairs, and heavy lifting?
  6. If we both work 40 hours a week, is the domestic labor split 50/50?
  7. If I am completely exhausted, will you pick up the slack without complaining?
  8. Do you expect a hot meal waiting for you every single night?
  9. How often should we realistically be doing laundry and deep-cleaning?
  10. Are you comfortable hiring outside help (maids/landscapers) if we can afford it?

Part 6: Intimacy & Expectations

  1. What is your expectation for the frequency of physical intimacy?
  2. If our sex life stalls, how will we communicate about it without causing shame?
  3. What are your hard boundaries in the bedroom?
  4. Do you believe flirting with other people is a form of cheating?
  5. What are your views on pornography consumption within a marriage?
  6. Do you believe that withholding intimacy is an acceptable form of punishment?
  7. How do you keep romance alive after the "honeymoon phase" ends?
  8. What is your primary "Love Language"?
  9. Do you need to go on regular date nights to feel connected?
  10. How do you handle rejection if I am not in the mood?

Part 7: Core Values, Faith & Lifestyle

  1. What role does religion or spirituality play in your daily life?
  2. How much alone time do you need each week to stay sane?
  3. Are we allowed to have close friends of the opposite sex?
  4. What are your views on alcohol and recreational drug use?
  5. Where do you want to be living in five years? (City, suburbs, country?)
  6. How do you view vacations? (Adventure and exploration, or resting on a beach?)
  7. If you get offered a dream job in another state, do we uproot our lives?
  8. What are your political views, and will our differences cause tension?
  9. Do you believe in having shared passwords and open phone policies?
  10. How much social media sharing is acceptable for our relationship?

Part 8: Health & Aging

  1. Do you have any genetic medical conditions I should know about?
  2. How do you prioritize physical fitness and diet?
  3. If I gain weight or age naturally, how will it affect your attraction to me?
  4. What are your end-of-life wishes? (Do you have a will/living directive?)
  5. If one of us becomes paralyzed or severely disabled, what is the plan?
  6. Do you believe in taking daily preventative medical care seriously?
  7. How do you respond to mental health struggles like depression or anxiety?

Part 9: Loyalty & The Hard Truths

  1. If I am accused of a crime, are you standing by my side or calling a lawyer?
  2. What is the biggest lie you have ever told to protect yourself?
  3. Do you believe that divorce is always an option, or is it absolutely off the table?
  4. If we lose everything tomorrow, will you still respect me?
  5. Have you ever cheated on someone in the past? Why?
  6. What makes you the most insecure about yourself?
  7. What is the one thing you are most terrified of me finding out about you?

Part 10: The Promise

  1. Why do you want to marry *me*, specifically, over anyone else in the world?
  2. What is the greatest strength I bring to your life?
  3. How do you plan to protect our marriage from outside interference?
  4. Are you ready to stop making decisions as an "I" and start making them as a "We"?
  5. Will you still date me after we are married?
  6. What is your ultimate definition of a successful life?
  7. Do you view me as your equal, your superior, or your subordinate?
  8. Are you willing to grow, evolve, and change your mind over the next 50 years?
  9. When the romance fades into routine, what will keep you anchored here?
  10. Are you absolutely certain that this is the best decision of your life?
  11. (100) If you had to describe our future together in three words, what would they be?
