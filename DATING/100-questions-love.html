100 Questions You Should Always Ask Someone You Love

• Relationship Diagnostics | Lucky Pikas Studios

Time does not guarantee intimacy. You can live in the same house with someone for ten years and never actually know who they are beneath the surface. True intimacy requires deliberate, consistent curiosity.

Whether you have been dating for six months or married for twenty years, asking deep, psychological questions prevents the relationship from stalling in the "Roommate Phase."

Here are 100 essential questions designed to bypass small talk and uncover the true values, fears, and desires of the person you love. Ask them on a road trip, during dinner, or on a quiet Sunday morning.

Part 1: The Inner World & Mindset

  1. What is the one accomplishment you are most secretly proud of?
  2. When do you feel the most completely "yourself"?
  3. What is a belief you held 5 years ago that you have completely changed your mind about?
  4. What is your biggest irrational fear?
  5. If you could rewrite one specific day in your past, which day would it be?
  6. What makes you feel instantly overwhelmed or anxious?
  7. How do you know when your social battery is completely drained?
  8. What is the hardest lesson you’ve ever had to learn the hard way?
  9. When you are stressed, do you prefer space, or do you prefer to be comforted?
  10. What is a dream you’ve given up on, and do you regret letting it go?
  11. If money were truly no object, how would you spend your Tuesdays?
  12. What is a compliment you received years ago that still makes you smile today?
  13. What is the most misunderstood thing about your personality?
  14. Do you consider yourself an optimist, a pessimist, or a realist?
  15. What is a habit you have that you secretly want to break?
  16. What does "success" mean to you now, versus what it meant in your twenties?
  17. Who in your life brings out the absolute best version of you?
  18. Who in your life completely drains your energy?
  19. What is a harsh truth you've had to accept about yourself?
  20. What are you most looking forward to in the next ten years?

Part 2: Childhood & The Blueprint

  1. What is your happiest memory from your childhood home?
  2. What did your parents teach you about money, and do you agree with them?
  3. What is the biggest mistake you watched your parents make in their marriage?
  4. Were you allowed to express anger or sadness openly as a child?
  5. Who protected you the most when you were growing up?
  6. What is a childhood hobby you wish you still made time for?
  7. Did you ever feel like the "black sheep" or the "golden child" of your family?
  8. What is something your parents did that you will deliberately *not* do with your own kids?
  9. What was the household rule you hated the most?
  10. If you could tell your 15-year-old self one thing, what would it be?

Part 3: Conflict, Apologies, & Boundaries

  1. How do you physically feel in your body right before an argument starts?
  2. Do you think it is better to go to bed angry, or stay up all night fighting?
  3. What is the hardest thing for you to forgive?
  4. What is your specific "tell" when you are upset but pretending everything is fine?
  5. When we argue, what is one thing I do that makes you feel invalidated?
  6. What does a genuine, sincere apology look like to you?
  7. Have you ever held a grudge against me that you never mentioned?
  8. How do you know when a friendship has run its course and needs to end?
  9. What is a firm boundary you have now that you didn't have five years ago?
  10. Is there an argument we keep having that you feel we’ve never truly resolved?

Part 4: Evaluating Our Relationship

  1. What was the exact moment you realized you loved me?
  2. What is the most attractive non-physical quality I possess?
  3. When do you feel the most appreciated by me?
  4. When do you feel the most taken for granted by me?
  5. What is one adventure or goal you desperately want us to achieve as a team?
  6. If you could change one aspect of how we communicate, what would it be?
  7. What is your favorite memory of us from our first year together?
  8. Do you feel like we balance each other out, or are we too similar?
  9. What is something I do that makes you feel incredibly safe?
  10. What is a weird quirk I have that you actually love?
  11. Do you feel like I prioritize your needs above my friends and family?
  12. How do you describe our relationship to other people when I'm not around?
  13. What is the biggest risk we have ever taken together?
  14. Do you feel like we spend enough quality time together without our phones?
  15. What is the most vulnerable you have ever felt with me?

Part 5: Romance, Intimacy & Passion

  1. What is something simple I do that instantly turns you on?
  2. What is a romantic gesture you miss from the beginning of our relationship?
  3. How has your definition of intimacy changed since we met?
  4. What is a fantasy or desire you’ve been hesitant to share with me?
  5. Do you feel like our physical connection is a priority in our household?
  6. What makes you feel desirable outside of the bedroom?
  7. How do you prefer me to initiate physical affection?
  8. What is your favorite part of my body?
  9. When do you feel the most romantically connected to me?
  10. What is something we haven't done in a while that you really miss?

Part 6: Money, Security, & The Future

  1. What is your biggest fear regarding our financial future?
  2. Do you feel like we are a united team when it comes to managing our money?
  3. What is one luxury you refuse to compromise on, no matter our budget?
  4. Where do you see us living when we retire?
  5. If we won the lottery tomorrow, what is the very first thing you would buy for us?
  6. Do you feel pressure to be the primary provider?
  7. How do you feel about the way we split the household responsibilities right now?
  8. What is an investment (time or money) we need to make in ourselves this year?
  9. How do you want us to be remembered by our friends and family?
  10. What does true, absolute freedom look like to you?

Part 7: Trust & Loyalty

  1. What is your absolute definition of cheating? (Is emotional infidelity just as bad?)
  2. Have I ever broken your trust in a small way that I didn't realize?
  3. Do you believe that people can fundamentally change their character?
  4. What is the one secret you would be terrified of me finding out?
  5. Do you feel entirely emotionally safe sharing bad news with me?
  6. If a rumor spread about me, would you defend me in public before asking me the truth in private?
  7. Do you believe complete transparency is required, or is privacy necessary in a relationship?
  8. What makes you feel insecure in our relationship?
  9. How do you know that I am fully loyal to you?
  10. If we hit rock bottom financially tomorrow, do you trust that we would survive it together?

Part 8: The "What If" Scenarios

  1. If we had to pack up and move to a foreign country tomorrow, where would we go?
  2. If you could instantly acquire a master-level skill, what would it be?
  3. If I got sick and needed long-term care, how would we restructure our lives?
  4. If you had to write a book about your life, what would the title be?
  5. If we could only eat one cuisine for the rest of our lives, what are we eating?
  6. If you were given one hour to speak to a deceased relative, who would it be and what would you ask?
  7. If you could go back and change your career path, what would you be doing right now?

Part 9: Deep Reflections

  1. What is the most beautiful thing about getting older with me?
  2. What is a piece of advice you often give but find yourself struggling to follow?
  3. Do you think we all get what we deserve in the end?
  4. Are you at peace with the bridges you’ve had to burn in your life?
  5. What is the most important life lesson you want to pass down to the next generation?
  6. When you look at me across a crowded room, what is the first thought that comes to your mind?
  7. What is the greatest act of sacrifice you have ever witnessed?
  8. If this was our last conversation, what is the one thing you would want me to know?
"To love someone is to remain eternally curious about who they are becoming."
