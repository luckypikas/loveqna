Welcome to the Diagnostic Lab. Stop guessing and start auditing.

We rely on diagnostics for our physical health and our vehicle maintenance, but we often leave our romantic lives to pure, unvetted emotion. These tests, quizzes, and flowcharts are designed to strip away the illusion of "chemistry" and reveal the hard reality of "compatibility."

Self-Audits vs. Partner Audits

Before you can safely diagnose a partner's toxicity, you must map your own blind spots. Our tools are broken into two categories: tests to analyze your own vulnerabilities (like the Savior Complex or Trauma Bonding), and tests to evaluate your partner's behavioral patterns.
