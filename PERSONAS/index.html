A.I. Personas & Voice Systems

• Digital Companions & Dojos | Lucky Pikas Studios

Welcome to the A.I. Persona Hub. This section provides the raw frameworks, writing styles, and Text-to-Speech (TTS) voice parameters you need to engineer hyper-detailed, unique Artificial Intelligence companions.

Whether you are building a personal digital assistant, a creative writing partner, or a "Sparring Dojo" to practice conversational boundaries, the prompts below will give your AI an authentic, human-like soul.

The Relationship "Dojo" Simulators

Can AI personas be used for tests and training? Absolutely. A "Dojo" is a safe, simulated environment where you can practice handling difficult relationship scenarios before you face them in the real world.

  • The Gaslighter Dojo: Practice holding your ground and setting firm boundaries against an AI programmed to use manipulative DARVO tactics.
  • The First-Date Simulator: Practice extracting red flags using our conversational vetting scripts in a low-stakes simulation.
  • The Deep Talk Simulator: Practice opening up and sharing vulnerability with a highly empathetic, non-judgmental AI companion.

Design Your Companion (The Core Questionnaire)

Before selecting a prompt from the pages below, use this simple 4-step questionnaire to define your AI’s baseline personality:

  • 1. The Worldview: Is your AI a Cynic (questions everything, highly protective) or an Optimist (encouraging, sees the best in situations)?
  • 2. The Tone: Do they speak in formal, clinical therapy-speak, or do they use raw, urban "barbershop/group chat" street logic?
  • 3. The Empathy Level: Do you want a "Fixer" (offers immediate logical solutions) or a "Listener" (offers purely emotional validation)?
  • 4. The Voice Resonance (For TTS): Do you want a deep, gritty, slow-paced baritone, or a light, bright, high-energy cadence?
