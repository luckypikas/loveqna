🔥 The LoveQnA Dictionary of Modern Romance 🔥

• A glossary of Classic, Modern & Forensic Relationship Terms

A

A Catch 🎣🔥

A person with long-term partner qualities; a keeper.

Abandon 🚪🏃‍♂️

Leaving someone emotionally or physically with no warning.

Best Friends 👫❤️

A deep platonic bond that may or may not have romantic undertones.

Big Mama 👑🍗

A woman who runs the household or relationship.

Bodacious 🍑💋

Bold, curvy, fine, and full of presence.

Booty Call 📞🍑

Late-night intimacy with no strings attached.

Butt‑Buddies 🍑🤝

Two people who hook up casually with no romantic expectations.

C

Catfight 🐱💥

A heated argument or fight between two women, usually over a man.

Charmer 😎💫

A smooth talker who uses charisma to attract romantic interest.

Chaser 🏃‍♂️🔥

Someone who actively pursues a romantic interest.

Cheater 🚩💔

Someone who violates the relationship agreement.

Communication 🗣️🔑

The foundation of a healthy relationship.

Cornball 🤡👞

A lame or awkward person who thinks they have game.

Crush / Crushing 😍💓

Strong attraction toward someone, often without a relationship yet.

Cuddle Buddies 🤗🛏️

Two people who meet mainly for affection and closeness.

Cupcaking 🧁💞

Sweet, soft, affectionate early-stage flirting.

D

Dame 🎩👗

An old-school word for a classy woman.

DARVO 🛡️🐍

Deny, Attack, Reverse Victim and Offender — a toxic manipulation tactic.

Dating 💘📅

Going out with someone to explore romantic potential.

Devotion 🕊️❤️

Loyalty and dedication to one person.

Dragging His/Her Feet 🐢⌛

Taking too long to commit or make decisions.

E

Emotional Support 🤗💖

Listening, uplifting, and encouraging a partner.

Entertainment Value 🎭🎤

Someone’s ability to keep their partner engaged and excited.

Exclusive 🔐❤️

Dating only each other, but not yet officially “in a relationship.”

Feeling Each Other Out 🤔💞

Exploring mutual interest and compatibility.

F

False Accusation 🚨🙅‍♂️

Lying on someone to make them look guilty.

Financial Hostage 💳🔒

A partner who controls money, housing, or transportation.

Friends With Benefits 😏🤝🔥

Friends who add intimacy without commitment.

G

Gaslighting 🕯️😵‍💫

Manipulating someone into doubting their reality.

Getting to Know Each Other 🗣️💞

Early-stage bonding and discovery.

Got Him/Her Trained 🎯🐕

Teaching a partner how to treat you right.

H

Hobosexual 🛋️💼

Someone who dates for a place to stay.

Hook‑Up 🔥⚡

A casual physical encounter.

Hottie 😍🔥

A very attractive person.

I

Innocent 👼💫

Naïve or inexperienced in relationships.

Intuition 👀✨

Gut feeling that something is right or wrong.

K

Kickin’ It 😎🎶

Casually spending time together to see where things go.

Kicked to the Curb 🚗🛑

Getting dumped or rejected.

L

Loose Ends 🕵️‍♂️🤷‍♀️

Unresolved relationship issues.

Long‑Term Relationship ❤️📆

A committed partnership with shared goals.

Love Bombing 💣❤️

Overwhelming affection early on to trap someone.

Love Interest 💞👀

The person someone is romantically focused on.

Low‑Key Relationship 🔒💑

A relationship kept private.

Lover’s Quarrel ⚡💋

A heated argument between lovers.

M

Married 💍❤️

A legally and emotionally committed partnership.

Monogamous 🔒💑

A relationship where both partners agree to be exclusive.

N

Narcissist 🪞👑

A manipulative partner lacking empathy.

One‑Night Stand 🌙🔥

A single encounter with no future expectations.

O

Open Relationship 🔓💑

Committed partners who allow outside connections.

Old Flame 🔥❤️‍🔥

An ex who still lingers in your mind.

P

Partners / Partnership 🤝❤️

Two people committed to building a life together.

Persuader 🧠❤️

Someone trying to convince another to give them a romantic chance.

Player 🎲😏

Someone who dates multiple people while hiding intentions.

Polyamorous 💞💞💞

Multiple consensual romantic partners.

Power Couple 👑💑🔥

Two high-achievers thriving together.

Prospect 🌱❤️

Someone who shows promise as a future romantic partner.

Pursuer 🏹❤️

Someone actively trying to win another’s affection.

R

Red Flag 🚩⚠️

A warning sign of toxicity.

Relationship Goals 🏆💑

A couple admired for their love and commitment.

Ride or Die 🚗🔥❤️

A fiercely loyal partner.

Romancer 💐😏

Someone who uses charm and affection to attract someone.

Running Game 🎮🃏

Using tricks or smooth talk to manipulate romantically.

S

Sidepiece 🍽️😉

A secret romantic or physical partner.

Simp 🤦‍♂️💸

Someone who over-invests in someone who doesn’t reciprocate.

Slow Fade 🌫️🚶‍♂️

Gradually disappearing instead of breaking up.

Slow Jam 🎶❤️

A romantic R&B song.

Sneaky Link 🕵️‍♀️🤫

A secret hookup.

Sweet‑Talker 🍯🗣️

Someone who uses flattering words to persuade romantically.

Talking Stage 🗣️💘

The early phase before official dating.

Target 🎯💘

The person someone is pursuing romantically.

Trying to Sweet‑Talk Someone 🍯😏

Persuading someone into becoming your partner, hookup, or sneaky link.
