🔥 The LoveQnA Dictionary of Deception 🔥

• A glossary of Classic & Modern Relationship Terms (From the 60s to Today)

Welcome to the official lexicon of LoveQnA. This living document defines the street logic, psychological tactics, and classic game used in romantic relationships. *We will update this dictionary as we go along.*

A - C

A Catch 🎣🔥

Synonyms: A Winner, A Keeper

Someone who has all the qualities you’d want in a long-term partner.

Abandon 🚪🏃‍♂️

Leaving someone emotionally or physically with no warning.

Best Friend 👫❤️

A ride-or-die type who may or may not have romantic ties. Some claim this status while secretly catching feelings.

Big Mama 👑🍗

Synonyms: Boss Chick, Head Chick in Charge

A woman who calls the shots in a family or relationship.

Bodacious 🍑💋

Synonyms: Luscious, Thick, Voluptuous

A bold, curvy, fine woman with serious presence.

Booty Call 📞🍑

A relationship of convenience where intimacy is the main focus—usually late at night.

Broad 🏠👜

Synonyms: Chick, Dame (old-school)

A casual term for a woman, sometimes affectionate, sometimes dismissive.

💬 Old-School Love Wisdom:
"If someone only hits you up at night, you ain’t in their heart—just in their schedule."

Catfight 🐱💥

A heated argument or physical fight between two women, usually over a man.

Cheater 🚩💔

Someone who violates the relationship agreement by stepping out with someone else.

Collar Around His Neck ⛓️🐶

A man who’s completely controlled by his partner.

Communication 🗣️🔑

The foundation of a healthy relationship. Bad communication = messy love life.

Cornball 🤡👞

A lame, try-hard, or awkward person who thinks they got game but really don’t.

D - G

Dame 🎩👗

An old-school word for a classy woman.

DARVO 🛡️🐍

Acronym: Deny, Attack, Reverse Victim and Offender.

A highly toxic manipulation tactic. When confronted with proof of bad behavior, the abuser denies it, attacks the person confronting them, and flips the script to make themselves the victim.

Devotion 🕊️❤️

Loyalty, love, and dedication to one person.

Dragging His/Her Feet 🐢⌛

Taking too long to commit or make a decision in the relationship.

💬 Classic Relationship Truth:
"Love moves at two speeds: fast when it’s right, slow when someone’s stalling."

Emotional Support 🤗💖

When one partner is there to listen, uplift, and encourage.

Entertainment Value 🎭🎤

A person’s ability to keep their partner engaged, entertained, and excited through:

  • Humor 😆
  • Great conversation 🗣️
  • Taking them dancing/movies/shows 🎶🎬
  • Telling good stories 📖
  • Gossip & playful discussions 🤫
  • Having an interesting life 🌍

Example: "He’s funny, charming, and always down for an adventure—his entertainment value is off the charts!"

💬 Entertainment Value Tip:
"People don’t stay because of looks or money alone. They stay because you make life exciting."

False Accusation 🚨🙅‍♂️

Lying on someone and making them look guilty.

Financial Hostage 💳🔒

A situation where one partner cannot leave the relationship because the other partner controls all the money, housing, or transportation.

Friends with Benefits 🔥👫

A casual relationship that includes intimacy but no commitment.

Gaslighting 🕯️😵‍💫

A form of psychological abuse where the abuser slowly manipulates the victim into questioning their own memory, sanity, or perception of reality.

Got Him/Her Trained 🎯🐕

When one partner teaches the other how to behave or treat them right.

H - K

Hobosexual 🛋️💼

A person who dates someone exclusively so they have a free place to sleep and do not have to pay rent.

Hottie 😍🔥

Synonyms: Stunner, Looker

Someone who turns heads wherever they go.

Innocent 👼💫

Synonyms: Sweet, Pure, Virgin

Someone naïve in relationships or unexposed to certain experiences.

💬 LoveQnA Classic Game:
"Game recognizes game, but real love recognizes real."

Intuition 👀✨

That gut feeling when something ain’t right—or when it is. The body's natural evolutionary defense mechanism.

Kicked to the Curb 🚗🛑

Getting dumped or rejected.

L - O

Loose Ends 🕵️‍♂️🤷‍♀️

Unresolved relationship issues or unfinished drama.

Love Bombing 💣❤️

An intense, overwhelming display of affection, gifts, and promises of a future together very early in a relationship, used strictly to trap and control a partner before the abuse begins.

Low-Key Relationship 🔒💑

A relationship kept private, either for personal reasons or secrecy.

💬 Real Talk:
"If you gotta keep your love a secret, you better make sure you ain’t the secret."

Lover’s Quarrel ⚡💋

A heated argument between people in love.

Narcissist 🪞👑

A partner who only cares about themselves, lacks empathy, and is deeply manipulative and controlling while demanding constant admiration.

Old Flame 🔥❤️‍🔥

An ex that still lingers in your mind.

P - S

Player 🎲😏

Someone who dates multiple people while keeping their true intentions hidden.

Promise of Devotion 💍💕

A vow to be loyal and dedicated in a relationship.

Red Flag 🚩⚠️

A warning sign that a person or relationship is toxic.

💬 Dating Wisdom:
"Red flags don’t turn green just because you ignore them."

Relationship Goals 🏆💑

A couple that others admire for their love and commitment.

Running Game 🎮🃏

Using smooth talk and tricks to manipulate someone romantically.

Simp 🤦‍♂️💸

A person who over-gives and over-invests (emotionally or financially) in someone who doesn’t reciprocate the effort.

Slow Fade 🌫️🚶‍♂️

When a partner slowly stops texting, calling, or making plans over the course of weeks, avoiding a direct breakup conversation entirely.

Slow Jam 🎶❤️

A romantic R&B song perfect for setting the mood.

Sneaky Link 🕵️‍♀️🤫

A secret, purely physical hookup that neither party acknowledges in public.

T - Z

Trauma Bond ⛓️🧠

A biological addiction to a toxic relationship created by "Intermittent Reinforcement"—where an abusive partner alternates between extreme cruelty and extreme affection.

Triangulation 📐😈

A manipulation tactic where a partner constantly brings a third person (an ex, a coworker, a friend) into the dynamic to make you feel jealous, insecure, and forced to compete for their attention.

Weaponized Incompetence 🧽🤷‍♂️

When a partner intentionally performs a task poorly (or pretends not to know how to do it) so that you will stop asking them to help and just do it yourself.

💬 Final Classic Wisdom:
"Love should be clear, not a guessing game."
