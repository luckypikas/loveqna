The Archetypes of Attraction: LoveQnA Lexicon

• Behavioral Dictionary | Lucky Pikas Studios

To navigate the dating market, you must accurately label the players. Some terms are rooted in clinical psychology (like Avoidant Attachment or Social Anxiety), while others are timeless street slang that perfectly describe a predator, a victim, or a savior. Here is the forensic breakdown of the most common romantic archetypes.

The Pursuers & The Hunted

  • Siren: A highly alluring individual who uses intense sex appeal, charisma, and mystery to lure partners into destructive or financially draining situations.
  • Chaser: A person addicted strictly to the pursuit. The moment they secure the target's affection, their dopamine drops and they instantly lose interest.
  • Pursuer: Someone who actively and intentionally initiates romantic contact, often driving the pace of the courtship.
  • Courter: A traditional, patient pursuer who relies on old-school etiquette, consistency, and formal dates to win a partner's trust.
  • Secret Admirer: A person who observes and desires a target from afar, often lacking the confidence or appropriate social opportunity to make themselves known.

The Players & The Scammers

  • Catfish: A person who fabricates a completely false online persona (using fake photos or life stories) to lure someone into a digital emotional relationship.
  • Romantic Scammer: A highly calculated predator who fakes romantic love entirely for the purpose of draining the victim's bank accounts or acquiring assets.
  • Con-Artist / Grifter: A master manipulator who builds profound, false trust. They don't just steal money; they steal your sense of reality.
  • Swindler: Someone who uses charm to cheat a partner out of money or property during the "honeymoon phase."
  • Pickup Artist (PUA): Someone who studies and applies mechanical, psychological tricks (like "negging") to bypass a woman's defenses for short-term sexual conquests.
  • Playboy / Playgirl: A charismatic, non-committal individual who openly enjoys the single lifestyle and dates multiple people without offering serious devotion.
  • Huckster / Chiseler: A low-level opportunist looking for a free meal, a place to crash, or a way to live off a partner's hard work.

The Street Terms & Titles

  • Simp: A person who over-invests emotionally, physically, and financially in a prospect who offers absolutely zero reciprocal commitment or respect.
  • Trick: A person who is willingly or blindly used for their money and resources in exchange for the mere illusion of romantic or sexual access.
  • Hoochie (Mama/Daddy): An overly provocative, loud, and attention-seeking individual who relies entirely on overt sexuality rather than substance or class.
  • Hoodrat: An unambitious dreamer and hypocrite who gossips, takes shortcuts, is cheap, wasteful, and constantly brings negative energy to the household.
  • Waffler: A person who cannot make a firm decision on who to commit to. They constantly bounce back and forth between two people (usually a new partner and an ex/babymama).
  • Buster: A party-crasher; someone who fundamentally brings the energy of the room down and emits low-level, insecure vibes.
  • Clown: A person who repeatedly embarrasses themselves by returning to toxic partners or making foolish, highly public relationship mistakes.

The "Status" Archetypes

  • Proverbial Woman: The ultimate standard (from Proverbs 31). A woman of profound competence, moral integrity, and independence who brings absolute peace and immense value to her household.
  • Trophy Wife / Husband: A highly attractive partner chosen primarily to boost the social status and public image of a wealthier, often older spouse.
  • Wifey / Wifey Material: A woman who exhibits the loyalty, domestic competence, and stability required for a successful long-term marriage.
  • Princess: A highly entitled partner who expects to be catered to, financed, and pampered without bringing reciprocal value to the table.
  • Mama's Boy: A man whose primary allegiance and emotional enmeshment remains with his mother, completely overriding his ability to protect or prioritize his romantic partner.

The Situational Titles

  • Sidepiece / Sidechick / Middleman: The hidden, secondary partner who accepts fractional attention in exchange for staying in the shadows of a primary relationship.
  • Paramour: A classic, formal term for an illicit lover, usually involved with a married person.
  • Friends-with-Benefits (FWB): A purely physical arrangement lacking romantic exclusivity. It almost always ends when one party catches feelings.
  • Booty-Call: A highly transactional, late-night request for sex, completely devoid of courtship or emotional connection.
  • John and Jane: The generic, anonymous buyer of intimacy, or a perfectly average, unassuming couple hiding their dysfunction in plain sight.
"To navigate the dating pool safely, you must know what is swimming in the water with you. Do not mistake a Siren for a Proverbial Woman, and never confuse a Con-Artist for a Provider."
